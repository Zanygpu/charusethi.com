05
Team Education for Banks & Fintechs

Here is the gap no one is talking about. 88% of financial institutions have committed budget to digital asset infrastructure in 2026. The investment decision has been made at board level, by the C-suite, often led by innovation teams. But the people responsible for communicating, positioning, and selling these products: your GTM, B2B marketing, content, and product marketing teams are routinely the last to be educated. They are expected to build narratives around compliance frameworks, tokenization mechanics, and stablecoin infrastructure without ever having had those concepts explained to them clearly.

These workshops close that gap. Practical, jargon-free sessions built for commercial and marketing professionals, not engineers. Covering the fundamentals of blockchain infrastructure, stablecoin mechanics, DeFi yield, regulatory context, and how to build credible brand narratives around digital asset products. Delivered as half-day or full-day engagements, onsite or virtual, for teams of 6 to 30.
